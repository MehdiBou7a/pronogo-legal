# Account Deletion — PronoGo

**Last updated**: June 6, 2026

[← Back to home](../) · [Version française](../delete_account_fr/)

---

This page explains how to delete your **PronoGo** account (an app published by **Mehdi Bouhaouala**) and the data associated with it.

## 1. Delete your account from the app (recommended)

1. Open the **PronoGo** app and sign in.
2. Open the profile panel (avatar at the top) → **Settings**.
3. Tap **"Delete my account"**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

Deletion is **immediate and permanent**.

## 2. Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, send an email to **mehdinparis.media@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "PronoGo account deletion". Your request will be processed within **30 days**.

## 3. Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is **permanently removed**:

- your sign-in account (email, credentials);
- your profile (display name, favorite team, level, XP, badges);
- your game data: cards, decks, predictions, progression, virtual currencies (PronoCoins, Golards), mini-games, mythic cards;
- your league participations and rankings (your name appears as "Deleted account" in past leagues);
- images you uploaded (league logos).

## 4. Data that is kept

The following is kept for a limited time, in line with our legal obligations:

- **in-app purchase (IAP) records**: kept for accounting, tax and anti-fraud purposes, for the legally required duration;
- **anonymized, aggregated usage statistics** (not linkable to you);
- technical backups are purged within **30 days**.

## 5. Contact

For any question about your data: **mehdinparis.media@gmail.com**.
See also our [Privacy Policy](../privacy_en/).
