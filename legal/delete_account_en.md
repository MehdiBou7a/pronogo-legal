# Account Deletion — PronoGo

**Last updated**: August 19, 2026

[← Back to home](../../) · [Version française](../delete_account_fr/)

---

This page explains how to delete your **PronoGo** account (an app published by **Mehdi Bouhaouala**) and the data associated with it.

## 1. Delete your account from the app (recommended)

1. Open the **PronoGo** app and sign in.
2. Open the profile panel (avatar at the top) → **Settings**.
3. Tap **"Delete my account"**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

Deletion is **immediate and irreversible**. Six categories of data remain nonetheless, for legal reasons or because they belong to other players: they are listed in section 4.

> ⚠️ **Uninstalling the app does NOT delete your account** or your data. Use the procedure above, or the email request below.

## 2. Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app (lost device, app uninstalled, forgotten password), send an email to **pronogo.dev@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "PronoGo account deletion". Your request will be processed within **30 days**.

## 3. Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is **permanently removed**:

- your sign-in account (email, credentials, including Google/Apple sign-in);
- your profile (display name, favorite team, level, XP, badges);
- your game data: cards, decks, predictions, progression, virtual currencies (PronoCoins, Goalards), mini-games, mythic cards;
- your league participations and your rankings — with one exception, described in section 4: your standings row in a league **still in progress** is kept, anonymized as "Deleted account";
- your referral links (friend code);
- the reports you filed, and your list of blocked players;
- images you uploaded (league logos);
- your push notification token.

Virtual currencies and unconsumed purchases are **lost without compensation** (see Terms of Service, section 6.3).

## 4. Data that is kept

Six categories remain — either because the law requires it, or because they also belong to the other players. These are exactly the six exceptions listed in our [Privacy Policy](../privacy_en/):

- **in-app purchase (IAP) records**: kept in a form dissociated from your game profile, for accounting, tax and anti-fraud purposes, for the legally required duration;
- **messages you already posted in a league that carries on without you**: erasing them would leave holes in other members' conversation. They are purged automatically after **30 days**;
- **your standings row in a league still in progress**: removing it would distort the result for the other participants. It is **anonymized as "Deleted account"** and disappears when that league ends;
- **your place in a finished season's hall of fame**: it is the shared history of the game, and rewriting it would change other players' standings. Only **already public** information appears there (display name, height reached, tier);
- **anonymized, aggregated usage statistics** (not linkable to you);
- **report files concerning a message you had posted**: they document the handling of a report filed by another player. The message itself is already gone (30-day purge); only the moderation record remains. The reports you **filed** yourself are deleted along with your account.

Finally, independently of these six categories, a copy of your data may remain temporarily in the **technical backups** of our hosting provider, Google Cloud Firestore. Their retention period is set by Google, not by us ([Google Cloud privacy notice](https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice)).
